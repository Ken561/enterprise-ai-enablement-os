# KPI Framework

## Purpose

Give executives (ED/MD, CIO/CTO) a consistent way to see whether the AI enablement program is working — not just how many people attended training.

## Metric Categories

| Category | What It Measures | Example Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | How many people the program has exposed to AI tools and training | Users with access, users trained |
| Activation | First successful use | % of trained users who complete a first task |
| Engagement | Recurring, sustained use | Weekly active users, repeat-use rate |
| Capability | Skill growth from training | LevelUp tier completion, self-reported confidence |
| Business impact | The outcomes adoption is meant to produce | Time saved, cycle-time reduction, quality improvement |
| Risk | Governance health | Open exceptions, incidents, unresolved review items |

## Reporting Principle

Reach and Activation are leading indicators — useful for spotting problems early, but not sufficient on their own to justify continued investment. Every executive report leads with Business Impact and Risk, with Reach, Activation, Engagement, and Capability as supporting context for *why* the business-impact number is moving the way it is.

## Cadence

Reported monthly at the portfolio level and quarterly at the strategy level, per the [Operating Cadence](../operating-model/operating-cadence.md). See the [Executive Dashboard](executive-dashboard.html) for a sample view of how these metrics are presented.
