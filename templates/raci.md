# RACI Template — AI Use-Case Lifecycle

R = Responsible · A = Accountable · C = Consulted · I = Informed

| Activity | Executive Sponsor | AI Program Lead | Product | Engineering | Security | Risk | HR / Talent Dev | Business Team | AI Champions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Submit use case | I | I | R | C | I | I | I | A | I |
| Triage & risk tier | I | A | C | C | R | R | I | I | I |
| Governance review (High risk) | C | R | C | C | R | A | I | C | I |
| Prioritize roadmap | A | R | C | C | I | I | I | C | I |
| Build training content | I | A | I | I | I | I | R | C | C |
| Deliver training | I | C | I | I | I | I | R | R | R |
| Support adoption | I | A | I | I | I | I | I | R | R |
| Report to executives | A | R | I | I | I | C | I | I | I |

## Notes

- Only one role is Accountable per activity — this template deliberately avoids shared accountability, which is the most common reason RACI charts fail to resolve real conflicts.
- Champions are Responsible for adoption support because they're closest to actual usage; the Program Lead stays Accountable because the program office owns the outcome.
