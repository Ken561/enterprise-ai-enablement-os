# Program Status Report Template

**Reporting period:** [Month/Quarter]
**Prepared for:** ED/MD and CIO/CTO stakeholders
**Prepared by:** AI Program Lead

## 1. Headline

One sentence: is the program ahead, on track, or behind, and why.

## 2. Business Impact

The metric leadership actually cares about — time saved, cycle-time reduction, quality, cost avoidance — with a one-line explanation of what drove the change.

## 3. Adoption Snapshot

Reach, activation, engagement, and capability metrics, per the [KPI Framework](../reporting/kpi-framework.md).

## 4. Risk & Governance

Open governance items, aging risks, and anything requiring executive attention or decision.

## 5. Pipeline

New use cases in intake, in prioritization, and moving toward launch this period.

## 6. Blockers Needing Executive Help

Anything the program can't resolve on its own — budget, sponsorship, cross-team dependency.

## 7. What's Next

Top 2–3 priorities for the next reporting period.

## Why This Structure

Executives read status reports to decide whether they need to act, not to review everything the team did. Leading with business impact and blockers — and putting activity detail last — respects that.
