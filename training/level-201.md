# LevelUp 201 — Applied AI

**Audience:** People already using AI tools regularly for non-agentic, single-turn or short-conversation work — developers, UX designers, program managers, business analysts.

**Goal:** Move from "I can get a usable answer" to "I can reliably get a good answer for my actual workflow, and I know why it worked."

## Illustrative Topics

- Structuring prompts for role-specific tasks (e.g., a program manager drafting a status report vs. a designer generating research synthesis)
- Iterating on outputs — refining rather than restarting
- Understanding grounding: why an AI-augmented research task connected to real source data behaves differently than one that isn't
- Evaluating output quality against a defined standard, not just "does this look right"
- Recognizing when a task actually needs a human decision, not an AI-generated one

## Completion Signal

A participant can take a real task from their own workflow and produce a materially better outcome using AI than they did before the course, and explain the tradeoff they made to get there.

## Format Notes

Includes a hands-on workshop component with role-specific exercises, in addition to core shared content — this is where the curriculum starts to differentiate by role while keeping a common foundation.
