# LevelUp 101 — AI Fundamentals

**Audience:** Anyone new to generative AI, regardless of role.

**Goal:** Build enough conceptual fluency and confidence that someone can use an approved AI tool safely and productively on day one.

## Illustrative Topics

- What generative AI is and isn't — capabilities and limitations in plain language
- Responsible use: data sensitivity, when not to paste something into a prompt, and why
- Writing a clear, specific prompt
- Recognizing and handling incorrect or low-confidence outputs
- Where to go for help and how to report an issue

## Completion Signal

A participant can describe, in their own words, one task they now do differently and why they trust (or don't trust) the output enough to use it.

## Format Notes

Designed for asynchronous, self-paced delivery through existing HR/Talent Development learning channels, with a short live Q&A option for teams that want it. Kept intentionally short — the goal is confident first use, not comprehensive theory.
