# Operating Cadence

> *Portfolio framework informed by enterprise AI program-management experience.*

## Purpose

A program is only as reliable as its rhythm. This cadence defines what gets reviewed, how often, and by whom — so status doesn't depend on someone remembering to ask.

## Weekly

- **Intake review:** New use-case submissions triaged and risk-tiered.
- **Blocker review:** Any use case stalled more than 5 business days is flagged for the Program Lead.
- **Adoption signal check:** Usage and support-ticket trends reviewed for early warning signs.

## Monthly

- **Portfolio review:** Full roadmap reviewed against capacity and sponsor priorities.
- **KPI review:** Reach, activation, engagement, and risk metrics reviewed against targets.
- **Risk review:** Open governance items and exceptions reviewed for aging and resolution.

## Quarterly

- **Strategy review:** Roadmap re-prioritized against updated business priorities.
- **Investment review:** Budget vs. spend vs. value delivered reviewed with Finance and FinOps.
- **Maturity assessment:** Program and individual use cases assessed against the [Adoption Maturity Model](operating-model.md#adoption-maturity-model).

## Why a Fixed Cadence

Ad hoc reporting creates two problems: leadership gets surprised by issues that were visible weeks earlier, and the program team spends unplanned time producing one-off updates instead of running the program. A fixed cadence turns reporting into infrastructure rather than a recurring fire drill.
